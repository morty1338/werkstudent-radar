About the data

Sources. Every morning a script collects all postings with “Werkstudent”, “Working Student” or “Werkstudierende” in the title from the public job search of the Bundesagentur für Arbeit, plus Werkstudent postings from companies' own career sites through the free Arbeitnow job API. A posting found in both counts once. LinkedIn, StepStone and Indeed don't allow automated collection and aren't used; many of their postings come from the same company career sites. Company career pages (section 06) are read directly from the public job boards of a list of companies in Berlin and München (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Personio).

Pay comes from the posting's salary fields or phrases like “17,50 € pro Stunde”. Each role counts once, and a median needs at least 10 roles.

Skills and German are found by rules with German and English synonyms, checked against 100 labelled postings. How accurate they are.

Your CV is read and analysed in your browser with the same rules. It is never uploaded or stored.

Job texts belong to the employers and aren't published here; links lead to the originals. Code and data: github.com/morty1338/werkstudent-radar.
